How We Started

A small family owned cafe for any and all to go after hours for studying, late night hangout or simply relaxing after a hard day at work. The inspiration to our establishment started during my college years our campus lacked any good studying spots for those late night essay writtings and doing so in the dorms I saw daily with a sleeping roomate isnt the most desirable solution. This cafe open after dark overlooking the quiet beach as opposed to the loud late night city goers is perfect for any. Whether you need a fresh enviornment, an escape or just can't sleep.
